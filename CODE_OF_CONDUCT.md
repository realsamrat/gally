# Code of conduct

Gally's issues, discussions and pull requests should be a place where anyone can ask a question or send a fix without being made to feel small.

## What's expected

- Be kind, and assume the other person means well.
- Critique the code or the idea, not the person.
- Explain your reasoning. "This is wrong" helps nobody; "this breaks when the grid has one column" does.
- Accept a "no" on scope. It's about the project, not about you.

## What isn't welcome

- Insults, harassment, or comments about who someone is rather than what they wrote
- Sexual language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private details
- Spam, including automated issues and pull requests nobody has read

## If something goes wrong

Use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment, issue or discussion. To reach the maintainer privately, open a report from the repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/realsamrat/gally/security/advisories/new) and say it's a conduct report.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block accounts that keep breaking these rules.
